CV Settings

How to use Google Drive for your CV

  1. Upload your PDF CV to your Google Drive.
  2. Right-click the file and select Share.
  3. Under General Access, change "Restricted" to "Anyone with the link".
  4. Click Copy link.
  5. Paste that link in the box below and click Save.

Alternatively, if you uploaded a file directly to your website folder, you can just type the filename (e.g., CV_ArceasJohnCalzada.pdf).
